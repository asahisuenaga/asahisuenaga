<p style="line-height:1.6; color: inherit;">Let’s be honest: staring at a flickering, thin black line all day is… well, <span style="color: #ff69b4; font-weight: bold;">soul-crushing</span>. Whether you’re grinding through a 20-page report, drafting your next novel, or hunting for your spot in a messy brainstorm, the standard cursor is a master of <span style="font-style: italic; color: #48c9b0;">hide-and-seek</span>. <a href="https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/rainbow-cursor-in-google/nnmghknojpihdnofejbocdcnmhibkfdc" style="color:#ffd700; font-weight: bold; text-decoration: underline;">Rainbow Cursor</a> extension is the <span style="background: linear-gradient(to right, #ffb6c1, #ff69b4, #da70d6, #9370db, #48c9b0, #f0e68c, #ffd700); -webkit-background-clip: text; -webkit-text-fill-color: transparent; font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.1em;">vibrant upgrade</span> for Google Docs you didn’t know you were missing.</p>

<h3 style="line-height:1.6; border-left: 4px solid #da70d6; padding-left: 10px; color: #9370db;">Features</h3>
<p style="line-height:1.6; color: inherit;">There isn't just one option; it’s a <span style="color: #48c9b0; font-weight: 600;">fully customizable caret</span>. Here is how you can make it your own:</p>

<ul style="line-height:1.6; list-style-type: none; padding-left: 0; color: inherit;">
<li style="margin-bottom: 12px;">🎨 <strong style="color: #ffb6c1;">Gradients:</strong> Move beyond solid colors. Choose from <span style="border-bottom: 2px dashed #ffd700;">20+ options</span> on the entire color spectrum.</li>
<li style="margin-bottom: 12px;">📏 <strong style="color: #da70d6;">Thickness:</strong> If the default line is too spindly, <span style="font-weight: 800; text-transform: uppercase; letter-spacing: 0.5px;">beef it up</span>. Increase the width for better visibility on high-res monitors.</li>
<li style="margin-bottom: 12px;">🧘 <strong style="color: #48c9b0;">Kill the Blink:</strong> Constant flashing can be a major distraction. Turn off the caret blink for a more <span style="color: #f0e68c; font-style: italic;">"zen"</span> writing environment.</li>
<li style="margin-bottom: 12px;">⌨️ <strong style="color: #9370db;">Typewriter Animation:</strong> Activate a <span style="font-family: 'Courier New', monospace; border: 1px solid #48c9b0; padding: 1px 6px; border-radius: 4px;">snappy 80ms</span> typewriter effect that makes every keystroke feel tactile.</li>
<li style="margin-bottom: 12px;">✨ <strong style="color: #ff69b4;">Translucent Effects:</strong> Want thickness without blocking text? Apply a <span style="opacity: 0.7; font-weight: bold; text-shadow: 1px 1px 2px rgba(0,0,0,0.2);">glass-like look</span>.</li>
</ul>

<h3 style="line-height:1.6; color: #ffb6c1;">Ready to Chase the Rainbow?</h3>
<p style="line-height:1.6; color: inherit;">If you're tired of the <span style="text-decoration: line-through; opacity: 0.6;">"same old, same old"</span> and want to add a <span style="color: #ffd700; font-weight: bold;">spark of joy</span> (and a lot of clarity) to your workspace, it’s time to give your Google Docs a makeover.</p>
